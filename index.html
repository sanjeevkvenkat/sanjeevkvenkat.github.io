Selected Work

Systems in production, each one running against real enterprise data.
Flagship

Agentic Governance Harness for Data Products

Most governance lives in documents nobody reads while building. This harness fixes that: ontology-driven agents working in production apply the firm's data governance standards automatically, at the moment data products and pipelines are being developed. They check data contracts, quality rules, lineage, ownership, and classification — then tell builders exactly what to fix, which DQ rules to add, and what's missing before sign-off.

Findings tracked through to implementation, sign-off on evidence instead of promises, production deviations caught and reported — every release leaves the data estate more trusted than it found it.

AI Data Product Readiness Assistant

The conversational front door to governed data. Producers and reviewers ask plain-English questions — "is this product ready?", "what's blocking sign-off?" — and get live answers drawn from governance scorecards, DQ results, lineage docs, and approval evidence, plus a draft briefing for the committee. Where the harness does the checking behind the scenes, this is the conversation on top of it.

Review meetings start from a shared, current picture instead of a slide deck assembled the night before.

Conversational AI for Risk & Investment Data

Plain-English assistants for risk managers, investment analysts, and data stewards — ask about data products, risk metrics, incidents, lineage, and controls, and get answers with context instead of another dashboard to decode.

Self-serve answers with explanations — fewer ad-hoc data pulls, faster decisions.
