Flagship

Agentic Governance Harness for Data Products

Most governance lives in documents nobody reads while building. This harness fixes that: ontology-driven agents working in production apply the firm's data governance standards automatically, at the moment data products and pipelines are being developed. They check data contracts, quality rules, lineage, ownership, and classification — then tell builders exactly what to fix, which DQ rules to add, and what's missing before sign-off.

Findings tracked through to implementation, sign-off on evidence instead of promises, production deviations caught and reported — every release leaves the data estate more trusted than it found it.
