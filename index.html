Flagship project

Agentic Governance Harness for Data Products

Most governance lives in documents nobody reads while building. This harness fixes that. It's an ontology-driven system where agents working in production apply the firm's data governance standards automatically — at the exact moment data products and pipelines are being developed.

As teams build, the agents check their work against data contracts, quality rules, lineage requirements, ownership, and classifications. They give immediate, specific feedback: which DQ rules to add, what's missing before sign-off, where a design drifts from policy. Every finding is tracked through to implementation. Deviations in production get caught and reported. Each cycle ends with a clear view of what improved and what's still open.

The loop closes: every release leaves the data estate more governed than it found it. The result is data products teams trust enough to actually adopt — safer, cleaner, and ready for production sign-off.

How the loop runs

1Agents evaluate the data product against governance standards as it's built
2Builders get specific feedback: DQ rules to add, gaps to close
3Implementation is tracked; sign-off happens on evidence, not promises
4Production deviations are caught, reported, and fed back into the next cycle
